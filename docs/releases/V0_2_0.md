# THE EYES 0.2.0 — The Second Observance

Six playable trial subsets, three match contracts, and a versioned Windows delivery for the local asymmetrical prototype.

## Download and play

Download **THE_EYES_v0.2.0_Windows_x64.zip**, extract the entire archive, and open **THE_EYES.exe**. Keep the accompanying files together. The Godot editor is not needed for this package. English and French quick starts, engine/font notices, build metadata and file checksums are included.

Begin with **Story** difficulty and **First Observance** to learn movement, cover and the Eye. Choose **Second Observance** to play the three new physical trials, or **Seeded Mix** for a repeatable combination.

## Three contracts in the same observatory

| Contract | Northwest | Northeast | South |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First Observance | Silent Countdown | Trial of the Echoes | Clockwork Relay |
| Second Observance | The Memory Vault | The Ritual Circle | The Clock Room |
| Seeded Mix | Silent Countdown or Memory Vault | Echoes or Ritual Circle | Relay or Clock Room |

Each round activates **three trials and requires three seals**. Seeded Mix chooses one trial per bay, allowing eight combinations; the same seed and game version reproduce the selection. The new apparatus occupies the existing connected arena. Its replacement interactions and props follow the active trial.

Both roles remain available: a survivor against the AI Eye, or the human Eye against four physical AI survivors. Gaze, cover, floor traps, shadows, four fixed observation sockets, character abilities and the escape rule continue to provide the match pressure.

## New trials

**The Memory Vault** asks you to identify four targets from six distinct relic shapes. Carry each real relic to its corresponding numbered cradle. A wrong cradle keeps your cargo; accepted placements remain locked. Multiple survivors cannot own the same relic.

**The Ritual Circle** uses a shared ember and five spatially separated vessels. Follow the seeded order, with protected checkpoints after two and four correct vessels. A wrong vessel rolls back the unbanked prefix and returns the ember. After all five are lit, occupy the **2.8 m radius ring for eight continuous seconds**. You can keep moving within the boundary to manage gaze pressure; movement itself does not interrupt the hold. Leaving resets the final hold, while the lit sequence stays complete.

**The Clock Room** provides three coupled levers controlling three hands with six positions each: A moves A+B, B moves B+C, and C moves C+A. Forward and reverse operations are exact inverses, and reset returns the hands to the same generated starting state. The generator uses a bounded shortest-path search, with 108 reachable configurations and 48 candidates at distance four; every generated puzzle requires at least four lever commands. Align the visible target, then activate the central seal press. A fleeting alignment alone does not complete the trial.

Tinker's existing **eight-second Overclock** now also offers the next legal clock lever/direction as a HUD hint, or the central press when already aligned. It leaves every move and final confirmation in the player's hands; it does not turn the mechanism or complete the seal automatically.

These are deliberately scoped implementations of three additional designs in the twenty-five-trial bible. The full production variants, additional realms and bespoke Eye powers remain future work.

## Controls and documentation

Survivor movement uses WASD / the corresponding physical ZQSD positions on AZERTY, or the arrow keys. Mouse looks, Shift sprints, Ctrl/C crouches, Space jumps, E interacts, and Q invokes the archetype ability. G safely returns a carried objective. At clock controls, E turns forward, R reverses, X resets from the centre, and E at the central press confirms alignment. Contextual station prompts and the in-game manual describe the active trial. The Eye uses the mouse to aim, 1–4 or the wheel to change sockets, left click for traps, right click to mark and Q to summon. Tab displays the map and Escape opens pause/settings or closes a trial panel.

The repository contains the expanded English bible, exact contract specification, source concept, original procedural assets, six-trial registry and CSV schemas, plus detailed Blueprint-only UE5 implementation recipes. The UE5 files remain a construction foundation: no completed Unreal map, compiled gameplay Blueprint assets or Unreal executable is supplied.

## Release verification contract

The publishing workflow uses the exact triggering source commit. It requires all of these suites under the pinned **Godot 4.7.2** Linux editor before packaging:

| Suite | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| `test_director.gd` | Original match-authority invariants |
| `test_world.gd` | Original arena physics, interactions and physical bot routes |
| `test_survivor_route.gd` | Original survivor completion route against active Eye AI |
| `test_trial_set.gd` | Contract selection and alternate-trial rule invariants |
| `test_second_world.gd` | Alternate apparatus, physical interactions and bot routes |
| `test_second_survivor_route.gd` | Second-contract survivor route against active Eye AI |

Godot error diagnostics fail these gates even when a process returns exit code zero. The editor download and Windows release template have pinned SHA-256 checks. Packaging validates the PE32+ AMD64 header and every ZIP member's CRC, and records the source revision and worktree state in `BUILD_INFO.json`.

The exact exported archive then passes to a **native Windows runner**. Its actual `THE_EYES.exe` starts headlessly as Survivor and Eye under each of First, Second and Seeded Mix. Each case must reach the requested playable scene, emit its readiness marker, exit successfully and produce no engine error diagnostics. Publication runs only after those six startup checks pass, and verifies that the tested archive's SHA-256 still matches the published file.

The published release body appends the precise commit, workflow run, archive checksum and actual platform result. **WINDOWS_SMOKE.json** records that evidence. **SHA256SUMS.txt** verifies the downloadable ZIP and smoke report; the archive has its own member checksums. This source document defines the gate and does not substitute for a successful run.

## Prototype limits

The game remains local only. Internet multiplayer, matchmaking, a full replay/partial-match save system, controller/remapping coverage, production rigs and the remaining nineteen trial designs are future work. The three original and three alternate trials are prototype subsets, not every feature of their production designs.

The Windows check is **native headless startup**, distinct from a graphical hardware playtest or complete interactive Windows playthrough. It does not measure GPU performance, validate every graphics driver, establish novice usability or certify difficulty balance. The executable is an unsigned development prerelease.

## Version preservation

The workflow targets `v0.2.0` and leaves `v0.1.0` untouched. If a `v0.2.0` release or tag already exists, it stops publication without replacing either. This is a workflow safeguard; it is not a GitHub-enforced immutability rule. Subsequent deliveries require a deliberate version bump. A separately rebuilt ZIP is not assumed to have the same bytes as the released archive.
