## Nine trials, four contracts

Third Observance adds three complete local observatory subsets drawn from the
original 25-trial catalogue:

| Bay | Trial | Playable rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Northwest / western route | T04 Light in the Darkness | Carry three numbered cells to matching refuge lamps, manage charge and align each lamp with a continuous hold |
| Northeast | T07 Strings of Despair | Intersect side and shape-family clues, then deliberately cut the uniquely indicated rope through three banked stages |
| South | T17 Chimes of Doom | Walk among four bells, perform two four-note phrases, and move the one shared damper to manage noise |

First and Second retain their existing six apparatus rules. Seeded Mix now
chooses one of three candidates per bay: **27 combinations**, always three seals.
The selection change means old Mix seeds can produce different sets in v0.4.
Same-version determinism and independent existing puzzle streams remain intact.
All four contracts support Solo, human Eye and human-plus-four-AI Squad.

## Light in the Darkness

Each survivor's lantern starts at 100 charge. Carrying a cell outside a refuge
drains 4 points/s; the permanent starting refuge and any lit lamp restore 12/s
within 3 m and clear sight. Empty charge prevents alignment but never walking,
returning to a refuge or releasing cargo. Each uniquely owned cell matches one
numbered lamp. Hold E still for 2 seconds within 1.8 m and clear sight to align it.
Every lit refuge is permanent and provides another recharge point. G or carrier
retirement returns the cell safely. Movement, damage, pause, release or loss of
reach/sight resets partial alignment, preserving already lit lamps.

## Strings of Despair

Four physical ropes have numbered shape tags: Circle, Crescent, Triangle and
Diamond. Every seeded stage has exactly one curved and one angular tag in each
pair. The clue identifies the left/right pair and the curved/angular family;
the intersection has one explainable answer. Hold E still for 2 seconds at the
chosen rope. A correct cut lowers the decorative cage and banks one of 3 stages.
A wrong cut engages a 3-second safety catch and emits noise; earlier stages stay
locked. Actors remain on the connected solid floor. A new stage changes the
shared clue, clears stale partial holds and requires a new deliberate operation.

## Chimes of Doom

Each bell has a number, shape, pitch and strike animation. The public score
shows a seeded four-note permutation. E at a bell strikes; R toggles the one
shared damper at that bell. Two phrases complete the trial. A wrong note resets
only the current phrase; the completed first phrase remains banked.

Correct, damped-correct and wrong strikes respectively contribute 6, 1 and 10 seconds
to the Eye's noise-memory mechanism at the actual bell location. Wrong strikes
remain loud even under the damper. This uses the existing Eye attention path,
not a newly implemented dedicated sound minion. Bell commands share a 0.45-second
interval; damper commands share 0.5 seconds. A bot observes each current score at
the real stand before using it, and re-evaluates stale actions when a human
advances shared progress.

## Cooperation and presentation

The Survivor Accord's orders, physical rescue, Keeper healing and ally spectator
continue across all new trials. Rescue has priority over a held apparatus action,
so one E input cannot rescue and align/cut simultaneously. Bot rescue or danger
response interrupts trial holds. Shared objective ownership prevents carrying a
cell together with a mote, relic or ember.

Survivors now have procedural arms, clothing details and role accessories, with
limb motion driven by their existing velocity. Compact world names preserve the
view of nearby clues. The roster separates health/class and task rows. The menu,
manual, objective prompts and controls describe all nine trial subsets. These
remain original prototype models; final production character assets are future
work. Controller physics and existing ability tuning are unchanged.
